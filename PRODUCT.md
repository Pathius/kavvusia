# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype of the homepage (this folder). The final product will be rebuilt as a WordPress + WooCommerce site (block or child theme) with a custom plugin, hosted on Polish shared hosting.

## Users
Home coffee drinkers ("domowi kawosze"): people who brew at home (pour-over, moka pot, espresso machine, French press) and want fresh, good coffee, but are not baristas. Polish-speaking.

## Product Purpose
Kavvusia is a small coffee roastery's online shop (palarnia kawy) — a university team project for the "Zarządzanie Treścią (CMS)" course at PJATK. Success: the site looks and works like a real commercial roastery shop; visitors can find a coffee that fits how they brew and what they like, and put it in the cart.

## Positioning
Warm, homely, unpretentious tone — "Kavvusia" is a diminutive ("kawusia"), friendly and humorous, without specialty-coffee snobbery.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Demo shop (legal requirement from the instructor):** the site must state clearly and visibly that it is a demonstration shop, a student project; products are not real, orders are not fulfilled, no payments are taken. Must appear site-wide (banner) and near cart/checkout/footer.
- Product search and filtering are important (instructor's note).
- Discount codes (WooCommerce coupons) should be demonstrated.
- Custom plugin (programming requirement): "Dobierz kawę" coffee finder — filters by roast level, brew method, taste notes.
- Content: blog/guides (poradniki), newsletter, standard shop pages.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Kavvusia. Logo: `ikona.png` / `logo.png` — rounded espresso-brown square, cream cup, two orange steam waves (the "vv").
- Logo colors: espresso #422212, roast orange #D77B33, cream #FCF0E1.
- Language: Polish.
- User dislikes serif display fonts (stated earlier in this project).

## Evidence on Hand
No real photos, testimonials, ratings, customers or press. All products and prices are fictional demo content. Do not fabricate reviews or claims presented as real; any numbers on the page are demo content.

## Product Principles
1. Feel like a real, lovable neighbourhood roastery — never a template.
2. Help a home brewer pick the right coffee quickly (method + taste first).
3. Warmth and humour over snobbery; plain Polish, no jargon walls.
4. Honest demo: the "sklep demonstracyjny" notice is part of the design, not an afterthought.
